Methodology Notes

  • Housebuilding rate = annual permits per 1,000 residents.
  • Real rent change = ZORI rent series deflated by CPI-U.
  • Population cutoff = 100,000+ (latest ACS).

Data Sources

  • U.S. Census Building Permits Survey (metro/CBSA).
  • Zillow Observed Rent Index (metro-level).
  • BLS CPI-U for inflation adjustment.
  • American Community Survey for population.
